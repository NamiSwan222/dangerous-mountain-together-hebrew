# Dangerous Mountain Together: Hebrew glossary

Source of truth for the Hebrew text in `translation/he/strings.json`. New or changed strings follow these terms.

## Style

- The player is addressed in the plural (בחרו, לחצו) for neutral gender.
- Menu items and buttons use nouns or infinitive-like nouns (יצירה, הצטרפות, מחיקה), not imperatives.
- Keep Latin for key names, acronyms and brands: FPS, X/Y axes, Steam, ESC/ENTER icons.
- A text that starts with Latin and must read right-to-left begins with U+200F (RLM), as in "FPS מקסימלי".
